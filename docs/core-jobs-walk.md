# The three core jobs, walked tap by tap

A walk of the built app on the screenshot seed (`pnpm dev:seed`), driven by a throwaway Playwright script: a phone (390 px, touch, Chromium) and a desktop (1440 px, keyboard). Taps count only what a person does in the app; the operating system's camera is "(+shot)". Counts "after" are what the proposed fix would give.

## Ranked friction, most first

1. **Search stops on a natural question ("PETG settings", "tugboat settings" give "Not a single strand").** Every word must match, and "settings" is not in the filler list of `src/shared/search-intent.ts` (neither are "setting", "profile", "parameters"), though it is the word people add when they look for what worked. "PETG", "PETG worked" and "what worked for PETG" are fine. Before: 1 tap + type + a dead end + edit the query (≥ 3 more actions). After: 0 extra. Cheapest fix: add the words to `FILLER`, with a test.
2. **A photo of the print at the printer becomes a new untitled idea unless you flip a segment.** The capture sheet always opens on "New idea" (by design), "This print" is the second segment, and Today's on-the-bed cards have no camera. Before: `+`, "This print", "Take photo", (+shot), "Add to print" = 4 taps. After (a camera button on each printing card that opens the sheet already aimed at that print, or the sheet opening on "This print" while something prints and the last capture was one): 2 taps + shot. Needs a decision, since opening on "New idea" is deliberate. With several prints the sheet names the newest one ("Towel hook, attempt 2"); there is no way to choose another.
3. **Tidying has no "move".** An untitled capture can get a title (tap card, tap title, type: 2 taps + typing) but ideas have no tags, and a photo or note captured as an idea cannot be moved to a build or attempt; the only way is deleting it and adding the photo again (≥ 8 taps, and the capture time is lost). Proposal: "Add to a build…" on an idea or build page: pick a build (recent first, the one on the bed on top), then optionally an attempt; photos and note move with an undo toast. After: 3 taps. The biggest change of the list.
4. **Logging a failed attempt (job b), 6 taps + typing.** `Log attempt`, `Spaghetti`, `Take photo` (+shot), tap the note, tap a tag, `Log attempt`. After the outcome the note is not focused (on desktop focus stays on the outcome buttons) and "What went wrong?" sits below the fold (the sheet body is 916 px in a 642 px window), so on a phone the tags are found by scrolling. The first tag chip ("Spaghetti") repeats the outcome just picked. Proposal: drop the duplicate chip when the outcome is Spaghetti, and move the tags above the photo row so they are visible without a scroll. After: 5 taps, no scroll.
5. **No keys for the outcome in the attempt sheet (desktop).** `A` opens it with focus on Worked, Tab goes to Partly, and 1/2/3/W do nothing; Enter ("logs it", says the footer) does nothing until an outcome is picked. Worked is the focused one, so only Partly and Spaghetti cost `Tab`s. Proposal: `1`/`2`/`3` (or W/P/S) pick the outcome in the sheet and are listed in `?`. Before: `A` `Tab` `Tab` `Space` `Enter` for spaghetti (5 keys); after: `A` `3` `Enter` (3).
6. **Finishing leaves you looking at the bottom of the page.** After "Finish build" the page stays scrolled where the button was, with the Done state and final photo out of view and the "Al dente" toast covering the last cards. Proposal: scroll to the top (`scrollBehavior()`) when the build finishes. Taps unchanged (1); one fewer manual scroll.
7. **Text that says too much.** "Started building" toast: "Off Up next. It stays in Ideas under Started." could be "Started. Undo". The attempt-2 sheet's "Starts from attempt 1's settings. Change only what you try differently." repeats the "Same as attempt 1" line under Settings.
8. **To check:** the search field's clear "×" looks blue in the phone shot (a native control?), off the palette.

## The jobs, as walked

**a. Snap a photo and a note at the printer.** Phone: `+` (or hold `+` for the camera), "Take photo" (+shot), tap the note and type, "Save idea" = 4 taps (3 without a note; hold-to-camera: 1 hold + shot + 1). Desktop: `C`, type (the note is focused), `Enter` = 2 keys. No waits: the sheet closes at once and the toast offers View. Good; the friction is the photo's destination (item 2).

**b. Idea → build → failed attempt → worked attempt → finish.** Phone: `Start building` (1), failed attempt (item 4: 6), second attempt `Log attempt`, `Worked`, `Log attempt` (3, settings carry over from the first), `Finish build` (1) = 11 taps + shot + typing. Desktop: `Start building`, then per attempt `A` + outcome + `Enter` (item 5).

**c. A week later, find what worked.** Phone: search icon (1, keyboard opens focused), type "PETG": results in about a second, grouped Recipes, What worked (each with settings and "Start again"), Builds, Attempts, Ideas, Spools: 1 tap + typing, settings visible without opening anything. Desktop: `/`, type. Fine unless the question has a filler word (item 1).

**d. Tidy up an untitled capture.** Title: tap card, tap title, type (2 taps + typing, autosaved). Tags and moving: not possible (item 3).
