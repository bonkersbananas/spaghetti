# 0070. Claude names an idea saved without a title, once, and a person's title always wins

Status: Accepted

**Decision:** An idea saved without a title and without a model link gets a short title from Claude (`claude-haiku-5-5` through the official `@anthropic-ai/sdk`, the same key as [0019](0019-claude-drafts-ideas-from-model-pages.md) and [0023](0023-claude-reads-a-print-from-its-photo.md)), read from its first three photos and its note, a few seconds after it is saved: plain words a person would write ("Spool holder for the shelf", "Cable clip, 6 mm"), in the note's language when it has words. Capture stays as it was, "Titles can wait": the idea shows at once as saved, and the title arrives in place through the change feed with a slow fade, on the cards, the idea page and the Journal. Ideas with a model link keep getting their title from the page's draft (0019).

**Never over a person.** The title is written only while the idea still has none, and the write is conditional in the database too: a title someone typed before or while Claude worked wins and the answer is dropped (the run says `named_by_person`); so does an idea that became a build meanwhile (`not_an_idea`). An idea is named once (one `item_titles` row per idea, ever): saving it, its first photo or the first words of its note start the job, whichever comes first with something to read. The idea page marks the title "Named by Claude" (a `ClaudeLine` under it) for as long as it still says what Claude wrote; an edit makes it the person's and the mark goes. There is no Undo: clearing the title or typing over it is the undo, and a cleared title isn't named again.

**Why Haiku:** a title is a few words from a glance at a photo or two. Claude Haiku 5.5 does it as well as Opus here, in a second or two instead of several, at a fortieth of the price ($0.10 / $0.50 per million tokens; a three-photo naming is about 5,000 tokens in and 20 out, a twentieth of a cent). It runs with thinking off and `effort: "low"`, and its answer is constrained to a JSON schema (`output_config.format`) with a null for "can't tell", which is better than a vague title; the Worker also drops stand-ins that say nothing ("Untitled", "3D print idea") and anything longer than 60 characters.

**How the call runs:** like the photo reads, after the response with `waitUntil`, so the bridge-less paths (the iOS Shortcut's capture endpoint) work too. A capture sends the idea first and its photos one by one after it, so the job waits 2.5 seconds before it reads the idea, then calls Claude with a 20-second timeout. The job row (`item_titles`) is the fallback: the 5-minute cron takes a job nobody has worked on for four minutes, twice at most, then marks it timed out. The row is also the record of the run (model, how many photos and how much note it read, stop reason, HTTP status or error type, time taken, never the photos or the note).

**Who and how much:** hidden without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (`GET /api/features` `titles`). A person's save and their sharing token (the `capture` scope alone) start it; an agent that can do more names what it saves itself. 60 namings a day for the workspace (rows with `ran_at`, UTC days). A refusal, a timeout or a failed call leave the idea untitled, with nothing on screen.

**Left out:** naming builds, asking again, a visible "naming…" state (the idea reads fine untitled while it waits), and server-side refusal fallbacks (Haiku has none; the idea simply stays untitled).
