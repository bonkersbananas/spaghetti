# 0039. The shopping list reads the Prisjakt pages people link, and suggests one through a search engine

Status: Accepted. Supersedes the "no prices" part of [0034](0034-shopping-list-links-not-prices.md); its other choices (one shared list, links, a hand-made order, Bought, Clear with Undo) stand.

**Decision:** when a thing on the shopping list has a Prisjakt product link (`prisjakt.nu/produkt.php?p=<id>`), the Worker reads that one page in the background and keeps its picture, the lowest price and the 1–3 cheapest offers (shop, price in SEK, in stock), with when it was read. The row shows the picture and "189 kr · Kjell · checked today"; the sheet shows the picture, the cheapest price, up to three offers as tappable rows that open the Prisjakt product page, and "Check prices again". Prices are always shown as found at a time, never as a promise. A pasted shop link (Kjell, Amazon…) gets a thumbnail from its page's `og:image`, only if that shop's robots.txt allows the page and the picture. A Prisjakt picture wins over a shop's.

**The boundary (what this feature does not do):**
- **No use of Prisjakt's own search, and no fetching of Prisjakt pages by Claude.** Prisjakt's search is disallowed by its robots.txt, so nothing queries it: "Find it on Prisjakt" stays a link that opens it in a person's own browser. The Worker never calls it either.
- **Finding the page: Claude sees a search engine's results, never Prisjakt's pages.** For a named thing with no Prisjakt link, the Worker asks Claude (`claude-sonnet-5-5`, the official SDK) with the web search tool limited to `prisjakt.nu` results, for "prisjakt.nu produkt <name>". Claude gets the name (and the shop link's address, if there is one) and receives only the search results' titles and addresses from Anthropic's search tool; it has no fetch tool, and nothing from a Prisjakt page is ever put in a prompt. It names at most one result, and the Worker accepts it only if it is a `prisjakt.nu/produkt.php?p=<id>` address that was among the results Claude was given. It is saved as a *suggestion*; the sheet shows "Is this it? Use it / Not this one", and nothing is read or filled in until a person says yes. "Not this one" is remembered. It is asked once per name (when the thing is added) and again only when someone taps "Look for it". It is hidden without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (`GET /api/features` `priceSuggestions`), and capped at 20 searches a day for the workspace; each run is recorded in `shopping_searches` (outcome and diagnosis, never the answer text).
- **No AI collection of page data.** Prisjakt's terms of use forbid automated collection beyond what robots.txt permits and name AI models. The prices, picture and offers are read by the Worker's own parser from the product page a person confirmed (JSON-LD and the offers in the page's embedded data), and no page text from Prisjakt or a shop goes to Claude.
- **Product pages only, within robots.txt.** The only Prisjakt address fetched is a product page (allowed) and its picture on `cdn.pji.nu`. The `go-to-shop` clickouts (disallowed) are never fetched or linked: offers link to the product page. For a shop, its robots.txt is read first (our name is `SpaghettiPriceCheck`); a robots.txt that can't be read means we don't read the shop.
- **Gentle.** After a person confirms or pastes a link, the page is read when the link is added or changed, when someone taps "Check prices again" (a second tap within 20 seconds does nothing), and at most once a day for things not yet bought (the 5-minute cron looks at three at a time). Bought things aren't refreshed. A few pages a day for two people.
- **Private use, no republishing.** Prices and pictures are kept for the two people of the workspace and shown only to signed-in people; nothing is published, exported or shared outside the app.
- **Fail quietly.** If the page's data format changes, a page is gone or a site refuses, the thing keeps what it had (older prices show their older "checked" time) and nothing else changes. A wrong match is fixed by pasting another link or clearing it, which also clears the prices and the Prisjakt picture.

**How it is kept:** `shopping_items` gained `prices` (JSON), `prices_tried_at`, `picture_source`, `picture_at`, `picture_tried` and the suggestion's columns (`suggestion`, `suggestion_claimed_at`/`_done_at`, `suggestion_tried`, `suggestion_rejected`), and `shopping_searches` holds the search runs; the picture bytes are one R2 object per thing (`shopping/<id>/picture`, as fetched: JPEG or WebP, no resize), served at `GET /api/shopping-items/:id/picture?v=<time>` (people only, cacheable because the address changes with the picture). The parser is `shared/prisjakt.ts`, the robots reader `shared/robots.ts`, the reading `worker/shopping/lookup.ts`, the search `worker/shopping/suggest.ts`. In e2e and screenshots the Worker reads a stand-in server (the `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL` origin: the fake Claude server answers the search and serves product pages) instead of the real sites.

**Why now:** a price list is only useful if it is at hand when you are about to order; opening Prisjakt for each thing was the chore. 0034 feared a block like Printables'; a test from Cloudflare's network (Nov 12) showed product pages are served to a Worker. The fact finding is in [the feasibility notes](../specs/prisjakt-lookup-feasibility.md).

**Consequences:** the suggestion costs a search and a few thousand tokens per named thing (roughly 1–3 cents, estimated, not measured; the cap bounds it at 20 a day). A suggestion can be wrong, which is why it is never used unconfirmed; it can also be missing, and the person then uses "Find it on Prisjakt" as before. the page's embedded data is an internal format and may change, which shows as prices that stop updating, not as wrong prices. If Prisjakt objects, or their terms or robots.txt change, turn the lookup off by removing the call in `routes/shopping-items.ts` and the cron hook; links keep working as before. No totals, quantities or price history.
