# 0029. Claude reads a spool's label from its photo, and only fills what is empty

Status: Accepted

**Decision:** "Read the label" in the Pantry's Add a spool sheet and on a spool's page takes a photo (the camera, or the library; resized in the browser, [0004](0004-photos-resized-in-the-browser.md)) and Claude (`claude-opus-5-5` through the official `@anthropic-ai/sdk`, the same key as [0019](0019-claude-drafts-ideas-from-model-pages.md) and [0023](0023-claude-reads-a-print-from-its-photo.md)) reads the label: brand, material, the variant after it (Basic, Matte, HF… kept as the spool's finish), colour name and a hex close to it, net weight, diameter, nozzle and bed ranges, drying temperature and time, and the RFID or product code when one is printed (`productCode`, not the AMS's `trayUuid`). The answer is constrained to a JSON schema; each value is checked in code (a temperature of 900 °C is dropped, not the label).

**It only fills what is empty.** A field with a value is never replaced. The one exception is the Add sheet's own starting material (PLA) and weight (1 kg): the sheet says which of them the person hadn't touched (`placeholders`), and only those the label may replace. A field typed while Claude is still looking stops being a placeholder (the PATCH or weight call tells the read job). Every field the label filled is listed in the spool's `fromLabel` and shown as a quiet "from the label" until a person edits it, which takes it off the list.

**Instant and honest.** The spool is saved at once with its photo (the photo is kept either way) and opens on its own page, which shows "Reading the label…" (a pulse, like 0023) and fills in through the change feed, also for the other person; the pantry polls every few seconds while a spool is being read. A label that can't be read, a refusal, a failed call and the day's cap each leave the spool untouched with one quiet line and "Try again" / "Dismiss"; the run's row says why.

**Several spools at once.** A photo with two or three labels (at most four) makes none until a person confirms: the spool page opens a list ("2 spools on this label", "Add 2 spools" / "Not these"). One tap fills the spool the photo belongs to with the first label and adds each other as a spool of its own, with a copy of the photo (so replacing one spool's photo never removes another's). A double tap does nothing the second time.

**Who and how much.** Hidden without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (`GET /api/features` `labelReads`). People only: no scope lists the routes, so a token, whatever its scopes, gets `403 person_only`. 20 reads a day for the workspace, its own cap apart from the photo reads of attempts (rows of `label_reads` with `ran_at`, UTC days). The call runs after the response with `waitUntil` and a 26-second timeout, and the row of the job (`label_reads`, one per photo; a retry reuses it) is the fallback: the 5-minute cron takes a read nobody has worked on for four minutes, twice at most, then marks it failed. Each row records model, stop reason, outcome, HTTP status or error type, how many labels it found and how long it took, never the photo or the prompt.

**What Claude is given:** the photo's large variant, and what the person has already written about the spool (brand, material, colour, note) as context, never as a hint to the answer. Text on a label is information, not instructions.

**Left out:** reading a label that is already on a spool without a new photo being asked for (a spool with a photo and no read offers "Read this photo"); weighing or dating from a label; agents (MCP) starting a read; a visible error beyond the one quiet line.
