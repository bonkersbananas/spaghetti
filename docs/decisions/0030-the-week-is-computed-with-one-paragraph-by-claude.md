# 0030. The week is computed, with one paragraph by Claude and one push on Sunday evening

Status: Accepted

**Decision:** A calm look back at one week (`/week/2026-11-09`, `GET /api/weeks/:monday`): the week's prints as photos first, then a few quiet lines (how the prints went, builds worked on and finished, ideas captured and by whom, comments exchanged, filament used, and for the current week what is waiting: Up next, a build whose last print was spaghetti, a spool running low, upkeep due). Every figure and line is computed from the notebook, never written by Claude, and works without the key. A week with nothing in it is one warm line, never an empty grid.

**A week** is Monday 00:00 to the next Monday 00:00 in the workspace's time zone, `Europe/Stockholm` (`WORKSPACE_TIME_ZONE` in `shared/week.ts`, a constant: the two of you live in one place, and the push and the page must agree whoever's phone asks). A print belongs to the week its attempt was **printed** in (`printedAt`, the "when" of the attempt, as in the journal), so a print started on Sunday at 23:30 and answered on Monday counts on Sunday; one answered later changes the figures of its own week only. Deleted attempts, builds, ideas, comments and photos are left out. An idea counts as captured by the journal's rule (not started within a minute of being made). What is waiting is a snapshot of now, so only the current week has it.

**Claude's paragraph** (`weeks` table, `worker/week/`): a few sentences in the app's voice noting a pattern the numbers don't say. The same key and model (`claude-opus-5-5`) as the drafts, the photo reads and the coach. It is written **once** per week, only for a week that is over or on its Sunday evening (from 19:00), and only for last week and this one, so paging back never spends the key; it is stored, and absent without the key, for an empty week, when Claude has nothing to add (null is allowed: a guess is worse than nothing), on a refusal or a failed call. It counts toward its own cap, 6 a day for the workspace (UTC days, not counted against the stand-in Claude). A visit to a finished week with no paragraph claims it before the response (the page says "Looking back at the week…" and picks it up when it lands); the Sunday push writes it first. It only says; it never changes anything.

**One push** (`worker/push/week.ts`, in the existing 5-minute cron, no new trigger): on Sunday between 19:00 and 22:00 Stockholm time, for each person with a device that has "Your week" on (a switch in the Notifications sheet, **on by default**, `push_subscriptions.week_nudges`), only when the week had at least one print or idea: "Your week: 4 prints, 1 spaghetti", opening that week's page. Once per person and week is the primary key of `week_pushes`, claimed before sending and released when nothing got through, so a late or overlapping run can't send it twice. Devices in their quiet hours wait and, since the window closes at 22:00, skip it.

**Ways in:** the push, a small "The week" link at each week's end in the Journal, and "This week" and "Last week" in the command palette. Nothing on Today.

**Left out:** a month or year digest (the Year page exists), writing for weeks older than last week, a per-person weekly summary, Claude changing anything, and sending when the week was empty.
