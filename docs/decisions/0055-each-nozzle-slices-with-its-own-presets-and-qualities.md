# 0055. Each nozzle slices with Bambu's own presets, its qualities keep their names, and the nozzle is a choice only when the two differ

Status: Accepted

**Decision:** Spaghetti slices for every nozzle size the X2D takes (0.2, 0.4, 0.6 and 0.8 mm) with Bambu's own X2D presets for it, in the engine's release since `slicer-v2` (`scripts/slicer/resolve-presets.mjs`): its machine, every process Bambu has for it, and a filament per material. The named qualities are each nozzle's own (`qualitiesFor` in `shared/slicing/settings.ts`): Fine and Standard are Bambu's processes (0.2 mm: Fine 0.08 High Quality, Standard 0.10 Standard, Draft 0.12 Balanced; 0.6 mm: 0.18 Balanced and 0.30 Standard; 0.8 mm: 0.24 Balanced and 0.40 Standard), Draft and Extra draft above Bambu's coarsest process are that process at a taller layer, Extra draft the tallest the nozzle prints (seven tenths of its width: 0.14, 0.28, 0.42, 0.56 mm). The settings keep a layer height in millimetres (decision 0047's shape); when the nozzle changes (`withNozzle`), a named height moves to the same name on the new nozzle (a model at 0.20 mm, Standard, slices at 0.10 mm for the 0.2 mm nozzle, and back), and a height without a name keeps its share of the nozzle's width (0.16 mm on the 0.4 mm is 0.08 mm on the 0.2 mm), within what the new nozzle prints.

Which nozzle: when the main and auxiliary nozzles fitted are the same size, nothing new shows (More keeps a size choice, for slicing ahead of a swap). When they differ, "Slice with…" offers them as a Nozzle group after the filament (so Quality, Strength and Supports keep a phone's first screen), each tile drawing the X2D's two nozzles with the chosen one solid, and the Printer page's slicing defaults offer the same choice for new models (`setup.slicing.nozzle`; defaults that name none, or name one that is no longer fitted, use the main nozzle). The card's settings row and its line name a nozzle that isn't the 0.4 mm. A filament Bambu has no preset for on a nozzle (the 0.2 mm has no carbon fibre, TPU, PA, PP or PCTG) slices with its nearest kin (PETG-CF as PETG, the others as PLA) and the sheet, the card and the story say so ("No 0.2 mm preset for PETG-CF: sliced as PETG."); silk is Bambu's own silk PLA there.

**Why:** Carl Oscar bought 0.2 mm nozzles (Nov 13) and asked for the other nozzle heads to be supported. Bambu's presets are tuned per nozzle (line widths, speeds, flow), so a 0.2 mm slice made from the 0.4 mm's presets would be wrong in every line; a quality name ("Standard") is what people mean, not a number that only fits one nozzle.

**Consequences:**

- The engine doesn't take a filament's nozzle mapping (`filament_map`; it maps filaments itself) and OrcaSlicer refuses two nozzle sizes in one slice, so a slice "for the 0.2 mm on the right" is an X2D with 0.2 mm nozzles: its toolpaths, time and weight are the 0.2 mm's, but the file doesn't say which of the two nozzles prints it.
- `presets.json` grew from 235 kB to 630 kB (41 kB gzipped), fetched once per engine version, only to slice.
- The `nozzle` problem (no presets for a nozzle) remains for an engine without them; the Rust slicer (session #775) adds the same nozzles on its side.
