# 0009. Filament is tracked in grams, automatically

Status: Accepted (built)

**Decision:** Carl Oscar chose to track filament in grams. A spool can be given a **net weight** (what it held when new: 1000 g for a Bambu spool, presets of 250, 500, 750, 1000 and 2000 g, or any number) and optionally an **empty-spool weight** (tare). Each finished attempt's slicer grams per filament, read from its .3mf, come off the linked spool; a failed print counts in proportion to how far it got. The levels (full, half, low, empty) follow from the grams.

**Why:** a level set by hand drifts and can't answer "is there enough for this print?". The slicer file already says how many grams a print needs, so the app keeps the count without anyone weighing spools, and a weighing is there to correct it.

## The rules

- **A ledger, not a counter.** Nothing keeps a running number. Grams left = the latest **weighing** (else the net weight) minus the usage of attempts finished after it. Usage is read off the attempts when asked (`src/shared/ledger.ts`, `src/worker/ledger.ts`), so editing an attempt's outcome, filaments or spools, deleting it, or undoing the delete simply gives a different answer. Weighings are the one stored part (`spool_weighings`); the net and empty weights are columns on the spool.
- **A weighing** is "on the scale: 812 g" minus the tare (or the grams left, entered directly). It takes effect at its time: an attempt finished before it is already in the figure and is not counted again.
- **Usage of an attempt** is each filament's `grams` (from the file; a lone filament with no grams of its own takes the attempt's `weightGrams`) for the spool it links with `spoolId`. It counts only once the attempt has an outcome, at its finish time. Worked and partly worked: all of it. Failed: in proportion to `failedAtPercent` when known, else all of it, and marked "about". A print still going, or off the bed with no outcome, has used nothing yet. A deleted attempt or build uses nothing; restoring it brings its grams back. Grams never go below zero.
- **Levels from grams**, as a share of the net weight (a spool weighed with no net weight counts as 1000 g): full from 70 %, half from 30 %, otherwise low, and **empty only at 10 g or less** (practically nothing to print with): 62 g is still a small print, so it is low. Full and half are the printer bridge's thresholds for the AMS's own percentage; low and empty by amount apply to grams only, and the hand-set and AMS levels are unchanged.
- **Running low** lists spools that are low or empty, spools at 100 g or less, and spools a print still on the bed needs more of than is left. "Copy list" copies it as text (the phone shares it through the share sheet where there is one).
- **Not enough for this print** compares each filament's grams with its linked spool's grams left, only for spools with gram data and filaments that say their grams, and only while the print has no outcome. It is a calm line beside the filaments (in the attempt sheet and on the attempt in the build), never a block.
- **No gram data, nothing changes.** A spool with no net weight and no weighing keeps its hand-set level exactly as before. Existing spools stay that way until someone sets a weight. New spools added in the app start at 1000 g (the sheet offers "Not tracked"); spools the bridge adds have no grams.
- **Grams win over the AMS.** With gram data the level is derived from the grams. A `remaining` sent for such a spool (the bridge sends the AMS's on every sync) is ignored and changes nothing, so the two never fight. Without gram data the AMS figure is the level, as before. The bridge program is untouched.

**Consequences:** `AttemptFilament.grams` is new (filled from the slicer file, dropped when "same as last time" carries settings over). Spool responses carry `netGrams`, `tareGrams`, `gramsLeft`, `gramsApproximate`, `weighedAt`, and `remaining` is derived; see [the API](../api.md). Not done: the bridge reading live grams from the printer, and a "Running low" line on Today (Today's layout is another piece of work). Printing live from the printer still isn't involved ([0010](0010-optional-read-only-printer-bridge.md)).
