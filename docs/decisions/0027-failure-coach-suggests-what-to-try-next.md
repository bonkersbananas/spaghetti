# 0027. A failure coach suggests what to try next, and only suggests

Status: Accepted

**Decision:** When an attempt is answered Partly or Spaghetti, Claude (`claude-opus-5-5` through the official `@anthropic-ai/sdk`, the same key as the drafts of [0019](0019-claude-drafts-ideas-from-model-pages.md) and the photo reads of [0023](0023-claude-reads-a-print-from-its-photo.md)) suggests two or three short, concrete things to change for the next try, each with a one-line why ("Add a brim: the stern lifted both times"). It is told the attempt (outcome, failure tags, note, what a look at its photo showed, settings, the spools' drying, the plate, the slicer file), the build's earlier attempts with what changed between them, what worked for the same material elsewhere in the journal (the latest successes and the recipes) and the X2D's limits and failure vocabulary from docs/printer.md. The answer is constrained to a JSON schema; a suggestion's settings are only ones the attempt sheet has fields for, checked on our side against what the printer has (nozzle at most 300 °C, bed 120 °C, chamber 65 °C, the plates and layer heights it offers) and dropped when the attempt already did it. What the sheet's fields can't say (a speed) is a short line for the next attempt's note.

**It only suggests.** Nothing is set on its own. On the build page the ideas sit under the failed attempt, marked quietly as Claude's; in the Log attempt sheet the previous attempt's ideas are "Use this" buttons, each with what it fills in told in the words of the attempt's changes ("added a brim", "bed 60 → 65 °C"), and a tapped one says so and can be undone. Dismissing one is one tap with an Undo toast, and stays dismissed for both people. An idea is never written into the attempt's own settings, note or tags except by that tap.

**Instant and honest.** Answering an attempt never waits. The job (`coach_runs`, one row per attempt, so an attempt is coached once) is made in the same request, so the attempt shows "Thinking about the next try…" at once, and the ideas arrive through the change feed (also for the other person). Nothing useful to say, a refusal or a failed call show nothing at all; the row's `run_outcome` says why. The call runs after the response (`waitUntil`, 26 s) and the 5-minute cron takes a job nobody is working on, twice at most, as with the photo reads.

**Who and how much.** Hidden without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (`GET /api/features` `coach`). A person's answer starts it, and so does a token with both the `attempts` and `printer` scopes (the bridge); other agents' answers don't, and dismissing is people only. 20 a day for the workspace, its own cap (rows with `ran_at`, UTC days), not counted while the Worker talks to a stand-in server (the e2e tests, screenshots and the seed), where it costs nothing. The suggestions are in the attempt's API object (`coach`), in a build's story.md and in docs/api.md.

**Left out:** coaching again when an answer is changed from Partly to Spaghetti, a visible error line, a settings change that has no field (the app records no speeds), keyboard shortcuts for taking a suggestion on desktop.
