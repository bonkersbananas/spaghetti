# 0040. The idea scout suggests three models once a week, and only suggests

Status: Accepted

**Decision:** Once a week Claude (`claude-opus-5-5`, the same key as the drafts of [0019](0019-claude-drafts-ideas-from-model-pages.md), the coach of [0027](0027-failure-coach-suggests-what-to-try-next.md) and the week's paragraph of [0030](0030-the-week-is-computed-with-one-paragraph-by-claude.md)) suggests three models to print, picked from what the two of you want and have done. Each is a MakerWorld or Thingiverse model with its link, its title, a picture and one short sentence on why it fits ("For the kitchen drawer you saved"). Nothing becomes an idea by itself: **Keep** makes an idea (the title, the link as its note and the picture as its first photo, then the existing draft-from-link path writes the rest, [0019](0019-claude-drafts-ideas-from-model-pages.md)) and **Not for us** puts it away for good: the scout never suggests that model again. Both have Undo (a toast, and on the week page a button), no confirmation.

**What it reads** (`worker/scout/context.ts`): each person's wishlist (never one kept private: the suggestions are shown to both), the ideas saved lately with who they are for, the builds and how they went (outcomes, failure tags, materials), the filament in the Pantry that isn't empty, and every model the scout showed before. Never a photo, a comment or a person's contact details. Titles and notes are given as information, never instructions. An empty notebook gets nothing: a guess is worse than nothing.

**What it never does:** make an idea, change one, or act on its own; fetch an arbitrary page. Claude's own server-side web search and web fetch are limited to `makerworld.com` and `thingiverse.com` (`allowed_domains`); Printables is left out because it blocks Workers (we can't check its links or pictures). Agents (MCP, tokens) may read the suggestions (`list_scout_suggestions`, `GET /api/scout`, scope `read`) and can never keep or dismiss one: those routes are person-only.

**Never a dead link.** Claude is asked for up to six, and the Worker checks each before anyone sees it: the address must be a model's own page (a MakerWorld `/models/<number>` or a Thingiverse `/thing:<number>`, not a search, a collection or a designer), not seen before, and the Worker's own fetch of it (the drafts' fetch: only those sites, redirects checked, public addresses only) must answer 200 with a page that has a title. The title and picture come from the page, not from Claude's words. The picture is downloaded only from the sites' image hosts (an allowlist, no redirects followed), at 900 px, and kept in R2 at `scout/<id>`. The first three that live are kept, those with a picture first; fewer is fine, none shows nothing.

**When and how much.** One Claude run a week: the 5-minute cron's first run after 07:00 workspace time (Europe/Stockholm) that finds no round for the current week claims it (`scout_rounds`, one row a Monday, claimed before the call, a quiet claim taken over once more). Monday morning rather than Sunday evening: the picks are "for this week" and read the whole week before it, and no new trigger is needed. There is no "Find three more" button: one run a week is the budget, and a hard cap of 3 runs a day (UTC) guards the bill. The run happens in the cron (it searches and reads pages, which outlasts what a request's `waitUntil` allows). A refusal, a failed call or links that are all dead leave the week quiet, with the reason on the round's row. Hidden without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (`GET /api/features` `scout`; every scout route answers 404).

**Where it lives.** A calm block on Today, "Three for you this week", drawn only while suggestions wait (the count follows: "Two for you this week"), and on the current week's page, where answered ones stay with a way back and an empty state ("The scout is out shopping") says what happens on Mondays. Photos first: three model pictures large, a swipeable row with the next card peeking on phones, three columns from `md`. The card is `ui/ScoutCard` (in the styleguide); the section is lazy.

**Memory.** `scout_picks.key` (`makerworld:1100001`, `thingiverse:6100002`) is unique over all weeks: a kept or dismissed model, and one already waiting, is never suggested again, and neither is a model whose link is already in an idea's note. A month after an answer the picture is removed from R2 by the daily cron; the row stays, because it is what the scout remembers.

**Tests** use the stand-in Claude (`scripts/lib/fake-claude.ts`): it suggests models only for a notebook that mentions "scout" (or when started for the screenshots), so other specs stay quiet; `scout-slow`, `scout-broken`, `scout-refused` and `scout-hollow` pick other answers, and an address with `dead` in it is the dead link (nothing is fetched from the real sites while a stand-in is set).

**Left out:** a "Find three more" button, Printables, a push notification, suggestions based on photos, and agents acting on a suggestion.
