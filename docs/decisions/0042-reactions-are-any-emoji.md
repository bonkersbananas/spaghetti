# 0042. Reactions are any emoji: a quick row, a full picker, and a line of them on the cards

Status: Accepted (supersedes [0038](0038-reactions-a-curated-six.md))

**Decision:** A reaction is an emoji, any one of them, in the system's colour emoji font (Apple's on the iPhones and Macs both of us use, as in Slack). The picker works like Slack's: the smiley opens a quick row of the emoji the two of us give most (six defaults, ❤️ 🤌 🤩 🔥 👏 🫠, until there is a history) and a "more" button that swaps in the full picker: search by name and keyword ("fire", "party", "pasta"), categories, your recent picks, a skin tone button. A phone gets a sheet with a large grid and the search under it, at thumb reach; desktop gets a popover with the search focused, arrows to move and Enter to pick. Pills are the emoji and a count when both of us gave it, yours tinted, with a tooltip saying who. The Ideas and Builds lists show each card's reactions as a quiet line of emoji (a count only where both gave the same one); they are not buttons, reacting happens on the idea or build.

The rules of 0038 stay: each person gives each emoji once per thing, giving it again takes it back; ideas, builds and comments; person-only (an agent's token can't give them); the change feed records an update of the thing reacted to. `reactions.kind` now holds the emoji itself; the Worker accepts exactly one RGI emoji (`\p{RGI_Emoji}`, at most 40 UTF-16 units) and answers 400 for anything else, except the six old names (love, chef, want, hot, bravo, melt), which still mean their emoji so a tab opened before the change keeps working. Migration 0037 turned the six old kinds into ❤️ 🤌 🤩 🔥 👏 🫠, so no reaction was lost. `GET /api/reactions/used` answers the most used emoji (both people) and your latest, for the quick row and "recently used".

**Data:** the picker's names, keywords and categories are Emojibase's English data, slimmed by `scripts/emoji-data.ts` into `public/emoji/en/` (served from our own origin, never a CDN) and fetched only when the full picker opens; the picker's code (Frimousse, a headless library, styled with our tokens) is a lazy chunk that loads when the quick row shows. Neither is in any screen's first load.

**Why:** Marie asked (Nov 12) for reactions to be seen in the list of ideas, for many more emoji and for Slack's style. Six glyphs in our own icons were warm but too few to say what we actually mean; with two people the pill still needs no names.

**Consequences:** emoji look different on a Linux machine without an emoji font (the screenshot and CI setup has Noto Color Emoji); we don't draw our own set. No notifications for reactions, none on attempts or photos, no custom emoji. Updating the emoji data means `pnpm update emojibase-data && node scripts/emoji-data.ts`.
