Capture

Snap it. Jot it. Done in ten seconds.

Failures

Spaghetti happens. Write it down.

The app is named after the classic failed print: the nest of loose filament waiting on the bed in the morning. Here a failure is a lesson with a photo. Tag what went wrong, and the next attempt starts from what you learned.
